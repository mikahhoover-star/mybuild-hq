Privacy information

Last updated: October 2026

MyBuild HQ uses Supabase to provide accounts and store home-building project information that you enter. Project information may also be saved in your browser for offline access and backup. You can export a copy of your project from the dashboard.

Payments are processed by Gumroad. Payment information is handled by Gumroad under its own privacy terms. MyBuild HQ does not need your full payment card number to provide dashboard access.

Your project may include budgets, contractors, materials, and property details. Please avoid entering sensitive personal information you do not need for your build. Account and project data are subject to the security controls of the services used, and no online service can guarantee absolute security.

For purchase or account questions, visit the MyBuild HQ Gumroad product page and contact the creator through Gumroad.

This page describes the current website functionality and may be updated as the service changes.
