MyBuild HQ usage information

Last updated: October 2026

MyBuild HQ is a home-building planning and organization tool. It does not replace professional architectural, engineering, legal, financial, inspection, or construction advice. Always confirm pricing, permits, safety, and building-code requirements with qualified local professionals.

Keep independent copies of important project information. Export backups from the dashboard regularly. While account sync is available, uninterrupted access and error-free storage cannot be guaranteed.

Product pricing and checkout details are shown on the Gumroad purchase page. Contact the seller through Gumroad for purchase questions.

This page provides preliminary usage information and is not a substitute for a creator-reviewed legal agreement.
