Frequently Asked Questions

Is MyBuild HQ a subscription?

No. MyBuild HQ is a one-time $24.99 purchase.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. It works in your phone's web browser as well as on a computer.

How do I access it after purchase?

After checkout, return to this site, create an account or log in, and follow the purchase verification instructions.

Can I back up my project?

Yes. The organizer includes export tools so you can keep a copy of your project data.
